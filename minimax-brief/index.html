05 / Reasonable questions

Before you press buy.

Everything important, minus fourteen pages of legal department confetti.

Get the first batch →
What comes in the box?

You get RIOT//01, all 48 launch games, a braided USB-C cable, a wrist loop, and a quick-start card. No subscription, no welcome-week discount loop, no plastic coasters.

Do I need an account?

Nope. Games, settings, and saves work offline. An account is only requested if you ever choose optional software updates or future cloud features—and the device is perfectly happy without one.

Can I play my own games?

Yes. RIOT Mode can load compatible homebrew from a microSD card. Official releases arrive through signed system drops; RIOT does not ship an open, cluttered game storefront.

How does multiplayer work?

Up to four RIOT//01 units can connect locally for supported games. It is designed for a sofa, a table, or one deeply concerning hallway race.

When will it ship?

The first 2,000 units are scheduled to ship in production order during the launch window shown above. We will confirm your place and color choice before taking payment.

What if I do not like it?

Return an unopened RIOT//01 within 30 days for a full refund. We also include a two-year limited hardware warranty for manufacturing faults that are not caused by competitive gravity experiments.
